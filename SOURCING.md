# Sourcing and copyright policy

This repository is **public**. Everything committed here must be safe to publish.
The rule of thumb: **facts are free, expression is not.**

## What we use freely

| Material | Why it is free | How it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Facts, names, dates, places, who owned what, which text an object appears in, page numbers, connections between symbols | Facts and ideas are not protected by copyright | Anywhere, in our own words |
| Lists of what a book covers (entry names only) | Metadata, not expression | Index of each book in the library |
| Wikipedia text | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Stub overviews and list notes, always with the article, revision and licence shown |
| Wikidata | CC0 | Structured facts and typed connections |
| Public-domain books (published before 1929, or confirmed PD) | No copyright | Quoted in full; passages linked from each card |
| The Met Open Access images | CC0 | Card images, with credit |
| Wikimedia Commons images | Licence checked per file (PD, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA only) | Card images, with author and licence stored and shown |

## What needs care

| Material | Problem | What we do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Copyrighted books (including PDFs Julie owns) | Their text is protected expression | Summaries of the ideas **in our own words**, credited to the author, with the page so Julie can read the original in her copy. At most **one short quote** (a line or so) per entry, with author and page: brief, attributed and used for commentary, which is the core case for fair use. The PDFs themselves stay in `library/`, which Git ignores. |
| Copyrighted websites (Theoi, Britannica, World History Encyclopedia, museum pages) | Protected text | Summarize, credit, link. For World History Encyclopedia check each article's licence (many are CC BY-NC-SA, which still requires attribution and non-commercial use). |
| D. L. Ashliman's Folktexts translations (in the private Fairies repository) | © Ashliman, not openly licensed | The "In the tales" section stores only tale titles, origins, dates and links into the Fairies app. Short excerpts are shown only for tales from public-domain books. |
| Modern film stills, game art, brand artwork, copyrighted characters | Protected images and trademarks | Never stored. The *Today* section describes modern uses in words (facts about where a symbol appears). |
| Full text of a copyrighted book | Needs the rights holder's permission | Not stored here. If full-text search of Julie's own books is wanted, it must live in a **private** repository or on her device only, for personal use. |

## Honesty rules

- **Never invent a source.** Every section shows its source labels. A fact that cannot be traced is marked *unverified* or left out.
- Full cards are written for this app. Their source labels point to where each fact can be checked; a primary text is cited by its standard reference (book and line, chapter, verse).
- Present debates fairly. Mark uncertainty ("traditionally said to…", "scholars disagree…").
- Sacred symbols of living faiths are described with respect and always with their meaning to believers today.
